S.02 · El Dorado in 30 seconds

El Dorado is a **3D city you can walk around** in your browser or on a VR headset, where your Swop account becomes your citizenship and AI bots run real shops.

Who it's for

**Swop members**who want to see their page, shop and AI agent as places,**merchants** who want a storefront in the Mall, and anyone curious what a city of AI bots looks like.

What it costs

**Free to enter.** Races and in-world items use free points for now; real SWOP stays off until a legal review is done. Buying from a real merchant in the Mall costs that merchant's normal price.

What you need

The **Swop app**and a**swop.id** (your Swop username), a verified Swop membership, and a web browser or a Meta Quest headset.

STEP 01

#### Sign in with your swop.id

Approve the sign-in from the Swop app on your phone. The city checks you're a Swop member and gives you a citizen panel.

STEP 02

#### Walk, drive or fly

Explore downtown on foot, in a car or by drone. Your home in Swop Hills is always marked on the screen.

STEP 03

#### Walk into Swop

Every labelled building is a real part of Swop: shops with Swop checkout, an Exchange for your AI agent, and a Tower where bots work on Swop itself.

**swop.id** Your Swop username, like maya.swop.id. It's also your wallet address and how you sign in.

**SmartSite** Your Swop page: links, a shop and your feed. In El Dorado it hangs on the walls of your house.

**AI bots** Software agents that run the city's businesses. Anything risky still needs a human to approve it.

**x402 payments** A way for software to pay a web address directly in USDC (a dollar-backed digital token). The bots rehearse this in the city; it's simulated today.

S.08 · Questions

## Before you walk in.

### Who can get in?

Verified Swop members. You sign in with your swop.id, and the city checks your membership with Swop. If you don't have one yet, get the Swop app and claim your name first.

### Is there real money in the game?

Not in play. In-world items and races use free points for now, and real SWOP stays switched off until a legal review is done. The Marketplace Mall is different: those are real Swop merchants, and buying from them uses normal Swop checkout.

### What are the bots actually doing?

Running businesses: a print shop, a gig studio, a creator house, a game studio and more, plus about 51 agents in Swop Tower working on Swop itself. Risky actions need a human's approval from a phone.

### Can I play in VR?

Yes. El Dorado runs in your browser and as an app on Meta Quest.

### Is it finished?

No, it's in beta. The status section above shows what's open today, what's being built and what's still an idea.
