Three things to know

## The fee, in three lines.

### You pay it, not the buyer.

Buyers pay your list price. Swop's 0.5% comes out of what you receive, with a minimum of $0.015 a payment.

Live on every Swop checkout

### AI agents: 0% at launch.

When an AI agent buys from your link, you get the full price in USDC. No platform fee while agent payments launch.

[How agents pay](https://www.swopme.co/pay)

### Card is separate.

Verified sellers can take cards too; card payments show their own fee at checkout, so this calculator counts crypto and agent sales only.

[Crypto vs card](https://docs.swopme.co/payments/crypto-vs-card)

Questions

## The fine print, plainly.

### Who pays the 0.5%?

You do, the seller. Buyers always pay your list price; the fee comes out of what lands in your wallet, with a minimum of $0.015 a payment.

### Why does my average sale matter?

Fixed fees and minimums hit small sales hardest. A $0.30 fee on a $2 sale is 15%; Swop's minimum on a $2 sale is $0.015. The calculator uses your average sale to count how many payments that is.

### Is the 0% for AI agents permanent?

It's the launch rate for purchases made by AI agents. If it changes, we'll say so here and in the app before it applies.

### What about card payments?

Verified sellers can accept cards. Card payments show their own fee at checkout, so leave card volume out of this comparison.

### Where do these "today" numbers come from?

From you. We don't assume any processor's pricing: type in the percentage and fixed fee you pay now. The results are estimates, not a quote.
