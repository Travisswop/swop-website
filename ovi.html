S.10 · Questions

Asked and answered.

Is Ovi available now?

Ovi is in beta inside the Swop app as your AI trading agent, and you can talk to it from Claude, ChatGPT or Grok through the Swop MCP. The standalone Ovi app for web, Mac and iPhone is still being built.

Can Ovi spend my money without asking?

Anything that moves money shows you the details and waits for your approval. On top of that, per-payment and daily caps apply, and you can switch AI spending off at any time in Settings → AI assistants.

Will Ovi make me money trading?

We don't promise returns, and Ovi doesn't either. It proposes positions inside the loss cap you set; markets can go against you.

What does it cost?

AI usage is paid in Swop Credits, funded with SWOP. Each answer costs what the model charges plus a fixed markup, and your credit balance shows at the top of the chat. For the standalone Ovi app, which is still being built, the planned rate is 1.2× the model cost.

Does Ovi learn from my chats?

No automatic learning from your chats. If Ovi trains its own models, it will only use examples people have separately agreed to share, and that consent is separate from keeping your chat history.
