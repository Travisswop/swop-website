Before you buy

The practical questions.

Does the person tapping need the Swop app?

No. NFC reading is built into iOS and Android — your profile opens in their browser. The app is only for you, to edit what the chip points at.

Which phones can read it?

iPhone 7 and newer read chips from the lock screen with no setup. Most Android phones from 2017 on read them the same way. For anything older, or a phone with NFC switched off, the QR on the sticker and stand covers it.

Can I change where a chip points after it's printed?

Yes, as often as you like, and the chip itself never changes. Reassign a team chip to a new hire, swap a seasonal menu, or point a sticker batch at a different campaign.

What about shipping and returns?

Orders ship within two business days. Free standard shipping in the US on orders over $50, flat $6 otherwise. Thirty days to return an unprogrammed chip for a full refund.

Can I put my own logo on it?

Logo printing is included on the Classic card and Team Kit, and laser etching is available on the Metal card. Upload artwork at checkout — we send a proof before anything is made.
