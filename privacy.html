Privacy & Policy

Effective date — October 29, 2026 (version 3.1)
01
Overview

This policy is provided by Swop Technologies Inc. (“Swop”), which operates swopme.co, the Swop app, and the Swop mobile apps and is responsible for (where the law uses the term, the controller of) the personal information described here. Swop does not sell or monetize user data for profit. Instead, data shared within the Swop ecosystem is used solely to improve products, foster community growth, and contribute to the public good of the Swop network.

02
What Data We Collect

Swop may collect: public blockchain activity (wallet addresses, transactions, NFTs, etc.); interaction data (likes, follows, posts, views, clicks, redemptions); metadata shared through Swop services (device/browser info, IPs, referral sources); and optional profile information you provide (email, name, avatar, etc.); sign-in identifiers processed by Privy, which provides Swop's login and embedded wallets; and what you send to Ask Swop, Swop's agent features, and SmartSite AI assistants, which AI models process to generate a response (see section 05); and, if you connect a third-party AI assistant to your account through the Swop MCP connector, the requests it makes and the actions it takes for you (see section 05). If you use El Dorado, Swop's virtual world at swopme.co/eldorado, Swop confirms your verified swop.id to it, and El Dorado keeps your public SmartSite information and your activity in the world. Swop Technologies Inc. is the controller of El Dorado data, as it is for the rest of Swop. El Dorado sets one essential cookie, ed_session, which keeps you signed in to the world; it is not used for advertising or tracking, and signing out ends it. Swop itself collects no biometric data, no card numbers, and no bank account credentials, and runs no invasive tracking. Card payments and seller verification are handled by Stripe, which does collect identity and financial details directly — see section 07.

03
How Data Is Used

All data is used to enhance platform features and performance, deliver personalized user experiences, identify and reward meaningful contributions, produce community insights, and improve fraud prevention and security systems. Swop may publish anonymized, aggregated data to promote transparency and ecosystem growth.

04
Data Ownership and Sovereignty

You own your data. Swop believes in data portability and user sovereignty. You can delete your account from account settings, and you can ask Swop to export or delete your data at any time by emailing support@swopme.co, or use the export option in the app where it is available. Swop responds within 30 days and provides exports in a common machine-readable format (JSON or CSV). Swop will not restrict access to the platform based on data preferences. The one exception is payment, refund, dispute, and seller-verification records: Swop keeps those for as long as its financial, tax, and legal obligations require, and cannot delete them on request while those obligations are still running.

05
Sharing with the Ecosystem

Swop may share de-identified data with verified contributors for ecosystem development, governance processes to shape platform direction, and analytics tools operated by Swop or its community. All sharing is done in alignment with Swop's goal to support public benefit, not private profit. Separately from that, Swop shares data with the service providers that operate the platform — cloud hosting (such as Amazon Web Services and Vercel), sign-in and embedded wallets (Privy), email delivery, live video, media storage, AI model providers for AI features, and payment processing, including Stripe for card payments and seller verification (section 07) — with swap, bridge, prediction-market, and perpetuals venues you choose to use, which receive your wallet address and order details, and where required by law. Swop does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

AI processing. When you use Ask Swop, Swop's agent features (such as trading agents), or the AI assistant on a SmartSite, the messages you send and the context the feature needs to answer (for example, relevant profile, wallet, portfolio, order, or market information) are processed by AI models to generate the response. Those models are run by third-party AI model providers or are open-source models that Swop hosts. The models Swop uses may change over time, and this section will be updated when they do. Currently, depending on the feature and the model it is configured to use, the providers are Anthropic (United States), OpenAI (United States), and DeepSeek. Each provider handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Third-party AI assistants. You can connect an AI assistant from another company (such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Grok) to your Swop account through the Swop MCP connector. You link it by signing in to Swop and approving the connection (OAuth); you never give the assistant your Swop password. Once connected, the assistant can ask Swop for the information its tools return, such as your profile, SmartSite, balances, tokens, orders, and positions, and that information is sent to the assistant's provider, which handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. Actions that move money only happen if you have turned AI spending on, and stay within the per-transaction and daily limits you set. You can turn AI spending off, change those limits, or disconnect an assistant at any time in Settings → AI assistants; disconnecting stops new requests but does not delete what the provider has already received.

DeepSeek is based in the People's Republic of China, and Ask Swop and the agent features can currently use it, so what you send to them may be transferred to, processed in, and stored in China, where data-protection law differs from the law where you live; see International transfers in section 08.

Please do not include sensitive personal information, passwords, private keys, or recovery phrases in messages to AI features.

06
No Third-Party Ads or Sales

Swop does not sell your data to advertisers or brokers. No third-party advertising networks are integrated into Swop products.

07
Card Payments and Seller Verification

Card payments are being introduced gradually and are not yet available to every seller. Where enabled, sellers can accept card payments alongside crypto. Card payments are processed by Stripe. This section applies only to card payments — crypto and x402 payments on Swop are not identity-verified and nothing here applies to them.

If you pay by card. You enter your card details directly into Stripe's own payment form on the web, or Stripe's payment sheet in the Swop mobile app, on app versions that support it. Those details go to Stripe, not to Swop. Swop never receives, processes, or stores your card number, expiry date, or security code, and does not store your card brand or last four digits either. Swop keeps the reference Stripe provides for the payment, the amount, the currency, and the status of the payment and of any refund or dispute. Your name and billing address, where Stripe collects them, stay with Stripe.

If you sell and accept card payments. Stripe verifies your identity before you can be paid and collects what it needs to meet its legal obligations — typically your legal name, date of birth, address, contact details, a government-issued identity document, in some cases a selfie taken for liveness checking, your business details, your tax identification number, and the bank account or debit card you want to be paid into. You provide some of it on Stripe's own hosted pages and some in the Swop app. When you provide it in the Swop app, your name, date of birth, addresses, phone, email, business details, and the last four digits of a national identity number pass through Swop's servers to Stripe and are not stored by Swop. Identity documents are uploaded from your device directly to Stripe, and your full identity number, tax identification number, and bank or card numbers are turned into single-use Stripe tokens on your device, so Swop does not receive those values. The app shows you the bank or card name and last four digits of your payout accounts, read live from Stripe and not stored by Swop. If you accept Stripe's terms in the app, Swop passes the date, IP address, and device details of that acceptance to Stripe.

What Swop keeps about a verified seller is a summary of the outcome, not the evidence behind it: whether you are verified, whether you can currently accept charges and receive payouts, your two-letter country, coded descriptions of anything Stripe is still waiting on, a reference used to look your account up with Stripe, and records of payouts sent to you — amount, currency, status, and arrival date, never the bank account they landed in.

Stripe's own role. Stripe handles this data for its own legal and regulatory purposes as well as on Swop's instructions, so Stripe's privacy terms govern what Stripe does with it. Read them at stripe.com/privacy. Deleting your Swop account does not by itself delete records Stripe is required to keep.

How long Swop keeps it. Payment records, verification status, and the underlying accounting entries are kept while your account is open, and afterwards for as long as they are needed to complete refunds and disputes, keep accurate financial records, and meet legal and tax obligations.

08
Your Rights, Transfers, and Age

Your rights. Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU and UK GDPR or California and other U.S. state privacy laws), you may have the right to know what personal information Swop holds, and to access, correct, delete, or receive a portable copy of it, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent where Swop relies on it, and to appeal a decision on your request. Email support@swopme.co to exercise them; Swop may need to verify your identity first. Swop will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights, and you may complain to your local data-protection authority. Data written to a public blockchain cannot be deleted by anyone.

International transfers. Swop and its service providers operate in the United States and other countries, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live. In particular, messages and context sent to AI features that currently use DeepSeek may be processed and stored in the People's Republic of China (see AI processing in section 05). For its other service providers, where the law requires it, Swop uses contractual safeguards such as standard contractual clauses where the provider offers them. Swop does not have such an agreement with DeepSeek: if you do not want your messages processed in China, do not use the AI features that rely on it, or contact us and we will tell you which features those are.

Age. Swop, including El Dorado and Swop's AI features, is only for people aged 18 and over, everywhere, and is not directed to anyone under 18. Swop does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If Swop learns that an account belongs to someone under 18, it will close the account and delete their personal information, except records it must keep by law (such as payment records). If you opened your account before October 29, 2026, this age requirement applies to you from November 28, 2026. To report one, email support@swopme.co.

Security. No system is perfectly secure, and Swop cannot guarantee the security of information sent to or stored by it.

09
Updates to Policy

Swop may update this policy. Each version is posted here with its effective date, which is never earlier than the day it is posted, and Swop will give notice of a material change on this site and, where it has your contact details, in the app or by email before it takes effect. Version 3.1 (October 29, 2026) corrects the same-day version 3: it names Swop Technologies Inc. as the controller of El Dorado data, describes El Dorado's sign-in cookie, explains how to export your data, and applies the 18+ requirement to accounts opened before October 29, 2026 from November 28, 2026. Version 2 replaces version 1 published earlier on October 7, 2026, which replaced the version effective May 21, 2025. Together they add how card payments and seller verification handle your data (section 07), name Swop's main service providers and the AI model providers it currently uses (section 05), name Swop Technologies Inc. as the company responsible for your information, and add section 08. The Swop app Privacy Policy also applies to the Swop app.
