Swop Pay, in plain words.

If you can share a link, you can sell. Here’s the whole idea on one screen.

What it is
A checkout that lives inside a link. You make a product once, and anyone who opens the link can pay for it.
Who it’s for
Creators, shops, freelancers, pop-ups: anyone with something to sell, online or across a counter.
What it costs
0.5% when a person pays (minimum $0.015), taken from your side so the buyer pays your exact price. 0% platform fee when an AI agent buys, at launch.
What you get
USDC, a digital dollar, in the wallet behind your swop.id. It settles in seconds, so it’s actually yours, not “pending”.
What you need
The Swop app and a swop.id. Card payments need a quick seller verification; crypto and AI agent payments don’t.

Every product is an x402 endpoint.

Non-browser requests get an HTTP 402 challenge, pay USDC on Base, and receive the order. Swop speaks x402 v1 and v2, settles through Coinbase's facilitator and lists stores for discovery. Agents can also buy through the Swop MCP.

S.10 · Questions

Asked and answered.

How much does Swop Pay cost?

When a person pays by card or crypto: 0.5% of the sale, with a minimum of $0.015. You absorb it, so buyers pay exactly the list price. When an AI agent buys, there’s no platform fee at launch: you receive the full price in USDC.

What is USDC?

A digital dollar. Each USDC is designed to be worth $1 and is issued by Circle. It arrives in seconds and settles straight into your wallet, which means it’s final rather than “pending” for days.

How do AI agents buy from me?

When an AI assistant or shopping agent opens your product link, it can pay automatically in USDC on Base and get the order back, with no account or checkout form. You don't need to set anything up: if you have a product, agents can buy it.

Do I need to verify my identity?

Not to take crypto or AI agent payments. Card payments need a quick seller verification first.

What do I get paid in?

Crypto and agent payments land as USDC in the wallet behind your swop.id. Card payments pay out through the card rail once you're verified.

What about physical products?

For things you ship, the payment is held in escrow until delivery and then released to you. Digital items deliver instantly.

Can I use it on my own website?

Yes. Take checkout on your own site, embed products, and receive signed webhooks (automatic messages to your server) for each order. The developer quickstart walks through it.
