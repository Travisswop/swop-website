A token you use, not one you wait on.

SWOP is the token used inside Swop. It pays for AI, it rewards things you do, like getting your trade copied or inviting a friend, and it's the currency of El Dorado. It isn't sold as an investment and nothing promises it goes up.

What it is
A token on the Solana blockchain with a fixed supply of 1 billion. It lives in your Swop wallet next to your other tokens.
Who it's for
People who use Swop: anyone using Ovi, traders whose trades get copied, people who invite friends, El Dorado players.
What it costs
Nothing to hold. Getting it with a swap carries Swop's 0.5% swap fee plus network costs. In the app today, AI usage is charged at the model's cost plus a fixed markup, in credits; the upcoming Ovi app is planned at cost plus 20%.
What you need
A Swop account. Your swop.id comes with the wallet that holds SWOP.

S.08 · Questions

Asked and answered.

What is SWOP?

SWOP is an SPL token on Solana with a fixed supply of one billion. Inside Swop it pays for AI, rewards activity like copied trades and referrals, and is the currency of El Dorado.

Does holding or staking SWOP earn a yield?

No. Swop doesn't promise any yield or return for holding or staking SWOP. Staking is described by what it lets you do, not by a rate.

How do copy-trade rewards work?

When someone copies your trade, you, the trader they copied, get 50% of that swap's fee back as a SWOP reward. The copy runs in the follower's own wallet.

How do referral rewards work?

For 12 months after someone joins with your invite, you get 50% of their swap fees, paid in SWOP.

What is planned for SWOP?

On Swop Chain, which isn't built yet: a stake creators can put behind their name, a bond for AI agents, gas fees, and staking for the chain's operators. Until it ships, it's a plan, not a feature.
