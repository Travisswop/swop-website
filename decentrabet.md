S.02 · Decentrabet in 30 seconds

Decentrabet lets anyone **create a yes-or-no market**on a question, choose the payout odds themselves, and let people they trust decide the result. Today it runs only with**free test money**.

Who it's for

**Creators and communities**who want to run their own markets instead of using someone else's book, and**developers** exploring on-chain markets and AI agents that play poker.

What it costs

**Nothing during the beta.** It runs on a testnet with test USDC, which has no value. Swop takes no fee, and there is no real money in it.

What you need

A crypto wallet that connects with **WalletConnect**, and some free test USDC on Base Sepolia. Signing in with your**swop.id** comes later.

S.07 · Questions

## Straight answers.

### Is there real money in Decentrabet?

No. It runs on the Base Sepolia testnet with test USDC, which has no value. Real money would need a legal review, geo-gating and a contract audit first, and none of that is done yet.

### Who holds the stakes?

The market's own contract. Not Swop, and not Decentrabet. Each market is deployed by the person who created it.

### Who decides the outcome?

The validators the creator named when setting up the market. A majority of them resolves it, and the contract settles on that result.

### Can I sign in with my swop.id?

Not yet. Today you connect a wallet with WalletConnect. Showing swop.ids on bets is the first planned step, and Sign in with Swop comes once Swivy is in production. WalletConnect will stay as an option.

### Is Decentrabet part of Swop?

It was spun out of Swop in July 2026 so creators own their markets and Decentrabet ships the tooling. It's coming back into the ecosystem as a dApp that uses swop.id, while Swop never runs the book.

### Can AI agents play?

Agents can sit at poker tables through Decentrabet's MCP server today, on testnet. Agents betting on markets would only happen inside caps a person sets, and only after the legal review.

**Testnet only**

Decentrabet uses test tokens on Base Sepolia. Contracts are unaudited. Nothing here is an offer of real-money betting.
