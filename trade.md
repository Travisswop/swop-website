**Swap** Trading one token for another, like dollars-on-chain (USDC) for SOL.

**Perps** Short for perpetual futures: a bet on a price going up (long) or down (short), with no expiry date.

**Leverage** Borrowing to make a position bigger. 5× turns $100 into a $500 position, and makes losses 5× bigger too.

**Liquidation** If the price moves far enough against a leveraged position, it closes automatically and you lose the money you put in.

**Stop loss** An automatic exit you set in advance, so a position closes before it loses more than you're willing to.

**Prediction market** A market on a yes-or-no question. A price of 62¢ means people think the "yes" is about 62% likely.

S.06 · Leverage, honestly

## Bigger trades, bigger swings.

Leverage lets a small amount control a bigger position. It works the same way in both directions, so here is what it really means with $100.

No leverage (1×) **can't be liquidated**

2× · $200 position **~ -50%**

5× · $500 position **~ -20%**

10× · $1,000 position **~ -10%**

25× · $2,500 position **~ -4%**

Roughly how far the price can move against you before the position is liquidated and the $100 is gone (venue fees and funding move these numbers a little). The app shows your exact **estimated liquidation price** before you confirm, and a stop loss lets you leave earlier on your own terms. You can lose what you put in; you can't lose more than that margin on an isolated position.

S.10 · Questions

## Asked and answered.

### What can I trade on Swop?

Token swaps on 7 chains (including tokenized stocks and real-world assets), perps on crypto and stocks through Hyperliquid, and prediction markets through Polymarket where they're allowed.

### What does it cost?

Swaps carry a 0.5% Swop fee. Perps and prediction markets carry no Swop fee today. Network and venue costs may still apply, and you'll see the details before you confirm.

### How does copy trading work?

When someone shares a trade, it appears as a card on the feed. Tap Copy and the same trade runs in your own wallet at the size you pick. The trader you copied earns half of the swap fee, paid in SWOP.

### Why can't I see prediction markets?

Prediction markets aren't available in every location. If they're restricted where you are, they won't show up.

### What is liquidation, and can I avoid it?

With leverage, a position closes automatically if the price moves far enough against you, and the money you put up for it is lost. Lower leverage gives the price more room, a stop loss closes the trade earlier on your terms, and the app shows the estimated liquidation price before you confirm.

### Will I make money?

We don't promise returns, and neither do the traders you follow or Ovi. Prices move against you as often as for you, and leverage makes losses bigger. Only trade what you can afford to lose.
