S.02 · In 30 seconds

## Geo Bucks, explained simply.

Think of a coffee shop's gift card, except the shop creates it in a few taps, it lives in your phone's wallet, and it can't be spent twice.

What it is

**Store credit a shop issues itself**, plus rewards ("drops") the shop places on a map for people nearby to claim.

Who it's for

**Local shops**that want regulars to come back, and**shoppers** who like being rewarded for showing up.

What it costs

**Swop covers the network fee** on Base when you launch. Payments through Swop checkout carry the usual 0.5% fee.

What you need

The Swop app and a swop.id. Shops also need a store address so drops and credit point to the right place.

For shops

No loyalty software subscription and no plastic cards.

For shoppers

### Walk by. Tap. Claim.

Credit from every shop you love sits in one wallet.

01 · LOOK

#### Open the map

Drops near you show up on it. If nothing's nearby, it says so.

02 · CLAIM

#### Tap, scan or walk in

Each drop is claimed the way the shop chose: NFC tap, QR, GPS or beacon.

03 · SPEND

#### Pay with credit

At checkout, your Bucks are applied and burned. Pay any rest as normal.

**Base** The public blockchain where Bucks live. It's fast and cheap, and Swop covers the network fee when you launch.

**Token (ERC-20)** The standard format for a digital currency on Base. Your Bucks are one, so any compatible wallet can see them.

**Burn** Destroying credit once it's spent, so the same Buck can never be used twice.

**Drop** A reward tied to a place and a time, like "free pastry for the first 20 people today".

**Voucher & receipt** Digital proof you claimed a drop or redeemed credit. Shops can reward people who hold them.

S.09 · Questions

## Asked and answered.

### What is a Geo Buck?

Store credit that a shop issues itself. Each Buck is worth $1 of credit at that shop's registered stores, and it sits in your Swop wallet next to your other money.

### Can I cash Bucks out?

No. Bucks are not redeemable for cash and are not guaranteed by Swop. Only the shop that issued them honours them, at its own stores.

### Who controls how many Bucks exist?

The shop. Each shop launches its own Bucks on Base and is the only one who can issue more. Swop holds no key that can mint a merchant's money.

### What happens when I spend them?

They're burned at checkout, so they can't be used twice. You keep a digital receipt, which the shop can use to reward regulars.

### How do I claim a drop?

Open the map in the Swop app, go to the drop, and claim it the way the shop chose: an NFC tap, a rotating QR code, being within range (GPS), or a beacon in the store. You get a digital voucher in your wallet.
