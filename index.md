## Questions

### What is Swop, in one line?

Swop is the social network where people, businesses and AI agents own their identity and move value. One swop.id gives you a page, a wallet and a way to get paid

### What is a swop.id?

Your name on Swop, like maya.swop.id. It’s your public page, people can pay it instead of a long address, and soon it will be your login for other apps.

### What does it cost to get paid?

Swop takes 0.5% of each sale (minimum 0.015 USDC). The buyer pays your list price, and we cover the network fee.

### Can an AI really buy from me?

Yes. Through the Swop MCP, assistants like Claude and ChatGPT can find your product and pay in USDC over x402, only inside limits their owner set

### How do I earn on Swop?

Sell anything with a product link, take tips on your posts, get paid by AI agents, and earn SWOP when someone copies your trade. Your page, posts and history stay yours

### Who controls my money?

You do. Nothing moves without your approval. Today wallets run on a partner; we’re building Swivy, our own wallet platform.

### What’s live and what’s next?

Live: swop.id, SmartSites, the wallet, checkout, trading, Swop Live and Swop in Claude and ChatGPT. Next: Ovi by text, Sign in with Swop, Decentrabet, then our own chain with a personal data vault
