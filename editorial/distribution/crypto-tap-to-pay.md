**Title:** How crypto tap-to-pay actually works (NFC + QR, no card needed)

**Body:**
> Quick answer: you tap your phone near an NFC terminal or scan a QR code, the wallet converts your crypto to the price shown, and it settles on-chain in a few seconds. No card, no cash, no separate conversion step.
>
> The part that varies by wallet/processor: what the merchant actually receives. Some setups pay the merchant crypto directly (their wallet, their custody decision). Others convert at the point of sale so the merchant is paid in fiat and never touches crypto. Same tap or scan at the register either way.
>
> Worth knowing before you use one: it is not reversible. Once the transaction confirms on-chain there is no chargeback, so double-check the amount before you approve.
>
> Full breakdown (NFC vs QR, fees, gas) with an FAQ: https://www.swopme.co/blog/crypto-tap-to-pay
>
> (I work on Swop — self-custodial wallet with an in-person payment feature, SwopPay, 0.5% checkout fee — mentioned it in the post, but the mechanics apply to any tap-to-pay setup.)
