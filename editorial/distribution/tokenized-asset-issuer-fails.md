## The one caption (posted verbatim everywhere)

> If a tokenized asset's issuer goes bankrupt, your token doesn't protect itself. What you recover depends on the issuer's legal structure, not the blockchain — segregated custody vs. just another creditor. Self-custody guards the wallet, not the issuer's promise. #RWA #crypto

Tokenized gold or Treasuries are only as safe as the issuer's bankruptcy structure — Paxos says PAX Gold sits in a bankruptcy-remote NY trust account, but plenty of other RWA tokens leave holders as ordinary unsecured creditors if the issuer fails.
