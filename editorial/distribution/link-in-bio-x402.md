### Option 1 — r/CryptoCurrency

**Title:** Web3 link-in-bio pages can now sell directly to AI shopping agents

**Body:**
> A "web3 link in bio" page is a profile link with a wallet built in — visitors buy directly on the page instead of getting redirected to a separate checkout tool. The new part: because these pages can now speak x402 (the protocol that repurposes HTTP's dormant "402 Payment Required" status code), an AI shopping agent can complete a purchase there too, no signup or checkout form involved.
>
> Mechanically it's the same request-response flow used for any x402-paid resource: the client requests, the server responds 402 with a price, the client signs a stablecoin payment authorization and retries, and the resource (or product) is handed over once it settles.
>
> On Swop this is live today: every SmartSite is an x402 storefront, and any product a creator adds becomes payable by AI agents in USDC, with payout going directly on-chain to the seller. Self-custodial, gas-sponsored, no separate merchant account.
>
> Full writeup + FAQ: https://www.swopme.co/blog/link-in-bio-x402
