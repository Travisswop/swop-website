## Discord / Telegram recap

USDC.e and USDC aren't the same asset — USDC is minted directly by Circle, while USDC.e is a bridged wrapper a third-party bridge created before Circle deployed natively on that chain. pUSD is a separate story again, a different stablecoin (not a USDC wrapper) that happens to share a similar-looking name. Full writeup, with the checklist for telling them apart: https://www.swopme.co/blog/usdc-vs-usdc-e
