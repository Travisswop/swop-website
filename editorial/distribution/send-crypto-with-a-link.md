### Option 1 — r/CryptoCurrency

**Title:** How "send crypto with a link" actually works (no wallet address needed)

**Body:**
> Most people still think sending crypto means copy-pasting a 40-character wallet address and hoping you didn't fat-finger a character. A bunch of apps now support a different flow: you fund a link (or QR code) instead of an address, share it however you'd share anything else, and the recipient claims it into their own wallet when they open it.
>
> Mechanically, your funds move to a pending/claimable state the moment you generate the link — either held at an intermediate address whose key is embedded in the link, or marked pending against a claim credential. Whoever has the working link can claim it, so treat it like a bearer instrument (a gift card code), not a casual text.
>
> Most implementations auto-expire and refund the sender if nobody claims it in time, though the exact window varies by app.
>
> Wrote up the full mechanics with an FAQ: https://www.swopme.co/blog/send-crypto-with-a-link
>
> (I work on Swop, a self-custodial Solana/ETH/Base/Polygon wallet that supports this — mentioned it in the post, but the mechanics apply generally.)

### Option 2 — r/solana

**Title:** You don't need someone's wallet address to send them crypto anymore

**Body:**
> Quick explainer on claim-link transfers, since it comes up a lot from people new to crypto: instead of entering a recipient's public address, you fund a shareable link. The recipient opens it, connects or creates a wallet, and the funds route there. No address, no chance of sending to a typo'd string that eats your funds forever.
>
> The catch is the security model changes: until it's claimed, the link itself is the claim on the funds, so anyone who gets it first — not just who you meant to send it to — can take it. Don't post one in a public thread.
>
> Full writeup with an FAQ: https://www.swopme.co/blog/send-crypto-with-a-link
>
> Disclosure: I'm on the Swop team (self-custodial wallet, gas-sponsored, Solana/ETH/Base/Polygon, supports send-by-link). Posting because the mechanics are useful regardless of which wallet you use.
