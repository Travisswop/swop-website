## Discord / Telegram recap (2 sentences)

A tokenized real-world asset is only as good as the redemption right behind it — Ondo's USDY/OUSG wrap Treasuries and BlackRock's BUIDL, Paxos's PAXG is 1:1 allocated London gold (redeemable physically above ~430 PAXG, cash below that), and a luxury-brand consortium tags real goods with on-chain provenance instead of offering redemption at all. New post breaks down what backs each one and where the honest limits are: https://www.swopme.co/blog/tokenized-real-world-assets
