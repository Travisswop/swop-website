## Discord / Telegram recap (2 sentences)

A wrapped token carries the same ticker and tracks the same price as the asset it represents, but it's minted by whoever locked the original — a bridge or custodian — not by the original issuer. New post breaks down how lock-and-mint actually works, where WBTC/wETH/wSOL fit, and what to check before trusting a wrapped balance: https://www.swopme.co/blog/what-is-a-wrapped-token
