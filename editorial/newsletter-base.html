01 — Fintech pulse
The SEC’s August: a purpose-built crypto rulebook, and custody rules headed to the White House.
The quiet story of the month is regulatory plumbing. The SEC’s proposed “Regulation Crypto Assets” — its first purpose-built framework for digital-asset offerings, with a safe harbor and targeted capital-raising exemptions — is in its comment window, and this week the agency sent its rewrite of adviser custody rules for digital assets to the White House for review. Under Chair Atkins the direction of travel is unambiguous: crypto is being written into the rulebook, not around it.
The private side is moving just as fast: Galaxy opened retail crypto-backed credit lines against BTC, ETH and staked SOL at 8.99% APR — borrow against the stack instead of selling it. Regulated rails plus credit against self-custodied assets is the grown-up version of this market taking shape. The takeaway for builders: distribution and custody are becoming the moat, and the rules are finally being written down.
