S.11 · Questions

Asked and answered.

What is a swop.id?

A name like maya.swop.id that opens your SmartSite, receives payments into your Swop wallet and signs you into apps built on Swop. It's one identity for people, businesses, bots and AI agents.

Is it a real ENS name?

Yes. ENS (Ethereum Name Service) turns names into wallet addresses, like a phone book. swop.id is an ENS name, and your name is a subname of it, looked up through a standard called CCIP Read. It's not an NFT, so there's nothing to mint or pay gas for. Wallets and apps that support ENS can look it up.

What does it cost?

The Swop app is free to download, and your name comes with your account. Premium ($8.99 a month or $59.99 a year) unlocks unlimited SmartSites.

What can I put on my SmartSite?

Links, a shop, a tip jar, a blog, forms that collect leads, a chatbot, booking, token-gated content, media and embeds. Your feed, holdings and tokens show as tabs.

Can people send me money from any chain?

Your swop.id points to wallets on Solana, Ethereum, Base, Polygon and Arbitrum, so people on Swop can pay your name and it lands in the right place. When sharing a raw address instead, only send tokens that match that address’s network.

Which apps can I sign into with it?

El Dorado and Chalk today, with Game Film AI next. Sign in with Swop for any app is being built on Swivy.
