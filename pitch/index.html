The problem

Today’s rails weren’t built for any of it.

Cards are slow and costly

Merchants pay a few percent per sale and wait days to settle.

Agents can’t hold a card

Software needs a native way to pay, with limits and a receipt.

Identity is locked away

Your name, followers and reputation live inside each platform.

Builders rent every layer

Wallets, RPCs and AI are billed per user or per call; margins cap out.

How money reaches a Swop store

Four rails. One checkout. One receipt.

LIVE

People pay in crypto

Any token in, USDC to the shop. Solana Pay, with EVM tokens routed across chains.

LIVE · US

People pay by card

Card, Apple Pay and Tap to Pay through Stripe Connect, for verified merchants.

LIVE

AI agents buy

Agents pay any Swop store in USDC on Base with x402. No account, no card.

LIVE

Your AI pays for you

Claude or ChatGPT pays an x402 link through Swop MCP, inside the caps you set.

Every rail ends the same way: order, receipt, payout and a signed webhook to the merchant. Shop credit (Geo Bucks) applies at crypto checkout.

The graph is the network

Every payment is a social edge.

  • Trades post to the feed; followers copy them in their own wallets
  • Copied traders earn half the swap fee, paid in SWOP
  • Referrers earn half their friends’ swap fees for 12 months
  • Shops issue their own credit and drops; receipts double as loyalty

More payments → a richer graph → more trust and more demand routed to the next payment.
