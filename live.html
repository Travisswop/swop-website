In 30 seconds

Live video, with a checkout.

Swop Live is live video inside the Swop app. You stream from your phone or browser, and the people watching can buy what you're showing without leaving the stream.

What it is
Live streaming built into your Swop profile, with your store one tap away for viewers.
Who it's for
Creators, shops and anyone who sells on camera. Teams and coaches who stream games get a pro camera app later.
What it costs
Going live is free. Sales use normal Swop checkout: a 0.5% fee (minimum 0.015 USDC) that the seller pays. Free accounts keep replays for 30 days, as the setup screen says.
What you need
The Swop app or a browser, and a swop.id. To sell, a product in your Swop store. Streaming software like OBS is optional.

STEP 03

Replay and restream

Your stream is saved as a replay. Restreaming (through Restream.io) sends the same video on to YouTube or Twitch at the same time.

RTMPThe standard way streaming software such as OBS sends video to a server. Swop accepts it, so pro setups work.
RestreamSending one live video to several places at once. Swop sends it to your Restream.io account, which passes it on to YouTube, Twitch and others.
ReplayThe recording of your stream, saved after you end it so people can watch later.
Pinned productAn item you put on screen during the stream; viewers tap it to buy without leaving.

S.09 · Questions

Asked and answered.

What can I stream from?

The Swop app on your phone, a browser, or streaming software like OBS over RTMP.

Can I stream to YouTube and Twitch at the same time?

Yes, through Restream.io: connect your Restream account and Swop Live sends your broadcast there, which passes it on to YouTube, Twitch and others while you're live on Swop.

Are my streams saved?

Streams are recorded as replays. If your connection drops and you reconnect, the pieces are stitched back together, and you can download the replay as an MP4.

How do viewers buy, and what does it cost me?

Pin a product and viewers pay through Swop checkout by card or crypto. Buyers pay the list price; you receive USDC on the buyer's chain minus a 0.5% fee (minimum $0.015) that the seller absorbs.

What is Streamy?

A pro producer app for iPhone and iPad with multiple cameras and a sports scoreboard overlay. It's still being built; when it's ready it will feed Swop Live.
